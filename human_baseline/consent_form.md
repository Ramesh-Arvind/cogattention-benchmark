## Purpose of the Study

You are being invited to participate in a research study that measures human
performance on a set of reading-based attention tasks. The purpose is to
establish a human baseline for a benchmark that evaluates cognitive attention
abilities in large language models (AI systems). By understanding how humans
perform on these tasks, we can better interpret and contextualize AI performance.

## What You Will Be Asked to Do

You will complete approximately 30 short reading-and-answering tasks presented
on screen. Each task involves reading a passage of text and answering one or
more questions about it. The tasks test different aspects of attention:

- **Tracking:** Following multiple items through a series of changes
- **Vigilance:** Finding specific targets scattered in a longer text
- **Filtering:** Extracting relevant information while ignoring distractors
- **Shifting:** Adapting to rule changes partway through a classification task
- **Detection:** Noticing unusual content while performing a primary task

The study is expected to take approximately 25--35 minutes.

## Risks and Discomforts

The risks associated with this study are minimal. You may experience mild
mental fatigue from sustained concentration. Some tasks involve reading longer
passages, which may require focused attention for several minutes. You are free
to take short breaks between tasks if needed.

There are no physical risks, and no sensitive or emotionally distressing content
is included. All passages are procedurally generated fictional text.

## Benefits

There are no direct personal benefits from participating in this study. However,
your contribution will help advance scientific understanding of how human
attention compares with AI attention capabilities, which may benefit the
broader research community.

- Data will be stored securely on encrypted servers for a minimum of 5 years
  following publication.
